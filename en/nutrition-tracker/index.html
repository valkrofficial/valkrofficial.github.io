Your nutrition. A clear daily balance.

What have you eaten, and what remains toward your goal? VALKR connects meals, calories and macros in one daily view, keeping training and nutrition in the same fitness app.

Several ways to log meals

Search for foods, scan product barcodes or use your own entries. Frequent meals can be logged again more quickly. Nutrition-label recognition reads packaging information; a plate photo estimates a prepared meal. These are different inputs with different sources of error.

Plate photo and fridge scan require VALKR PRO, an internet connection and your explicit permission for AI processing. These features are implemented in the current product. The public app launch is still ahead.

See calories and macros side by side

The daily balance shows consumed calories, your target and the remaining amount. Protein, carbohydrates and fat have separate values, making it clear when remaining calories coincide with an unmet protein target.

Calorie targets and workout energy are estimates. You can adjust goals; optionally including training energy changes the daily balance. Missing nutrient data stays visible instead of presenting incomplete logs as a complete measurement.

More daily context, fewer separate lists

Water and caffeine can also be logged. Weight history shows changes across multiple entries. Together with training, these create a personal record; a single meal or weigh-in does not explain that record on its own.

VALKR supports a personal fitness overview. It does not provide medical nutrition therapy or promise a specific weight change or laboratory-level accuracy. The values shown come from a demo profile.
