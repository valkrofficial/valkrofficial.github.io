Your workout tracker. Every set counts.

What did you achieve last time? VALKR connects your training plan, active session and history. Log strength training at the gym or at home and see your previous performance while you train.

A workout log that follows your session

Record load and reps for weighted exercises. Other movements use seconds, distance or the unit that fits the exercise. The active set row shows your target and previous performance; a rest timer supports the transition to the next set.

A typo does not have to remain until your next workout: completed working sets can be corrected. The exercise overview shows where you are in the session. Once finished, the workout becomes part of your history rather than just a temporary checkmark.

Gym and bodyweight training in one app

Barbells, dumbbells, machines and bodyweight movements are part of the current product. Open an exercise to inspect movement, working muscles and technique cues. The available visual depends on the exercise; not every movement uses the same video format.

The tracker suits people who want to record actual sets and compare them later. If you need a structure first, plan sessions or assemble your own. Training experience and available equipment matter more than a one-size-fits-all routine.

Turn logs into traceable progress

History and personal records show what has changed. VALKR also uses training data to inform targets; the rules differ between guided plans and custom workouts. More weight is therefore not a promise for every new session.

The app is preparing for its public Android launch. Screenshots show the current workout interface with a demo profile. The waitlist keeps you informed about launch; it is not an immediate app download.
