Calisthenics with a traceable path.

From bodyweight workouts to skill practice, VALKR connects exercises, a training plan and logged performance. Calisthenics is one training direction within the app, alongside strength training with weights.

Start with suitable variations

Pull-ups, push-ups, dips, leg movements and core work have different requirements. Guided onboarding takes your level into account. Sessions show concrete set targets; repetitions or hold times can be the relevant performance measures for bodyweight training.

A harder variation is not automatically the right next choice. The app uses performance data for progression guidance and lets you adapt exercises. When a new variation does not fit yet, a traceable record matters more than an impressive exercise name.

Keep skills distinct from your base session

The skill area contains steps for advanced bodyweight movements such as handstands or muscle-ups. Prerequisites and progression stages provide a reference. The app does not promise a skill within a fixed time or a camera-based technique assessment.

Exercises include movement guidance, muscle information and technique cues. The available visual depends on the entry. These tools support understanding and do not replace an in-person assessment of a difficult movement.

Connect bodyweight and gym training

If you supplement calisthenics with weights or machines, you do not need separate training lists. Custom workouts can combine movements from the library. Records, history and strength ranks show different aspects of your logged performance.
