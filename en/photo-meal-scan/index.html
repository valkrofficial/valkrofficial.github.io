A prepared meal is not always easy to find in a food search. VALKR offers a plate-photo scan for an editable estimate and a fridge scan for suggestions from your ingredients. Both features are part of the current product.

Plate photo: identify, correct and log

Choose a photo or take a new one. After processing, VALKR shows identified ingredients, estimated quantities, calories and macros. The result indicates uncertainty; a photograph cannot reliably measure hidden oil, ingredients or portion sizes.

In the result sheet, change quantities and remove incorrectly identified items. The meal is logged after your confirmation. Values are a practical estimate, not an exact weighing record. For known products or weighed ingredients, search and custom entries are useful alternatives.

Fridge scan: turn ingredients into an idea

Fridge scan uses a photo of available food and your remaining daily macros to suggest a dish. It returns ingredients, quantities and preparation steps. The suggestion is a starting point for review; it does not verify freshness, food safety or allergens.

Plate photo and fridge scan solve different problems: the first helps log food that is already prepared; the second helps you decide what to make from available ingredients. Both connect with the food diary.

What photo scans require

Scans are PRO features and need internet. Before the first AI analysis, the app asks for your explicit permission. The photo is transmitted for processing. The public privacy policy describes that processing; there is no blanket claim that all app data always stays solely on your device.
