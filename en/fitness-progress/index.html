See what changes through your training.

Compare exercises across multiple sessions

Depending on the exercise, follow repetitions, loads or hold times. Personal records mark your best logged performances. The strength overview groups performance by movement pattern so progress is not limited to your favourite exercise.

A useful comparison needs suitable logs: different technique, equipment or exercise variations can change the numbers. An estimated one-rep max is a calculation, not a tested maximum lift. Personal logs are separate from a general promise of results.

Strength ranks as a personal reference

The rank system runs from BASE to APEX. Strength, skills and regular training contribute to your current rank; activity and nutrition add context. Individual movement patterns have their own values. The app also shows what contributes to your rank.

Your rank is motivation within VALKR. It is not a medical fitness assessment, an official competition standard or a leaderboard of other users. Point progression also accounts for consistent training weeks.

Follow body metrics and everyday habits

Weight, measurements and progress photos complement training data. Weekly overview, streak and weekly story show consistency. You choose which photos and body metrics to record. A missing entry is not a measured zero.

Example screens use a demo profile. VALKR is preparing for its public Android launch; joining the waitlist provides launch information. It does not promise immediate access or a specific physical change.
