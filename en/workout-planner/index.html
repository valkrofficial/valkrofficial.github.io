A workout plan that fits your life.

Training days, experience and equipment vary. VALKR combines guided training plans with a workout builder, so you can follow a structure and still adapt exercises and sessions to your circumstances.

Start with guidance or build your own workouts

Your training level, chosen direction and available days inform the plan at onboarding. The weekly view shows scheduled sessions. Before starting, inspect exercises, set targets and estimated duration.

For a custom session, choose exercises from the library and arrange their order and sets. Templates such as full body, push/pull/legs or upper/lower provide starting points. A custom workout and a guided plan are different paths; their targets are not presented as the same automatic system.

Account for equipment and adapt exercises

VALKR supports equipment profiles for the gym, home and travel. These selections help you assemble a session from exercises you can actually perform. If a machine is occupied or unavailable, you can swap a movement.

The exercise library connects selection with muscle information and technique guidance. A plan is more than a list of names, although an on-screen explanation does not replace an in-person technique assessment. Choose movements that fit your ability and circumstances.

Keep planned and completed training connected

Open the workout interface directly from a planned session and log what you actually achieve. In guided plans, previous performance can influence later targets; reduced-load periods and retests are also part of the plan structure.

A plan is guidance, not proof of a training outcome. VALKR shows its current product before the public Android launch. The sample view uses demo data; you can currently join the waitlist.
