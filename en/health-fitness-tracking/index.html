See steps against your own target

Set a daily step goal and review your history. The source depends on the device. Health Connect can supply daily values from compatible Android data sources when the relevant data exists and permissions have been granted.

The website does not count steps or read watch data. A browser cannot fully test the native Android connection or its real measurements. Connecting is optional; not every phone or watch provides every supported data type.

Show sleep and recovery with their source

Depending on permissions and the data source, VALKR can import sleep, resting heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV). Personal reference ranges need multiple suitable entries. When data is missing, a manual sleep entry may provide a different basis; it is not a measured night.

Recovery, readiness and the stress-day indicator are calculated references. They do not detect diseases or injuries. Values from different watches and measurement conditions are not directly interchangeable. An unusual app value is not a medical finding.

You choose whether to connect

The Android app requests the required permissions. You can select data types and disconnect again. Photo scans, Ask VALKR and an enabled cloud backup have their own processing paths and must not be equated with entirely local logging.

The app is preparing for its public Android launch. Join the waitlist for launch information. The values in your overview depend on your permissions, your logs and the data supplied by your connected device.
