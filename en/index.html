Ranks

Every rep
builds your rank.

Your rank is built from strength, consistency, skills, activity and nutrition — and each of six movement patterns is ranked on its own. No leaderboard. Just you against your last set.

9ranks from BASE to APEX, three divisions each
6movement patterns — push, pull, row, press, legs, core
+35LP more room for every week with two or more training days — a rank can't be rushed
